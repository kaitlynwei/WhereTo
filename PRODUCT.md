# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite, a small Node API layer, Firebase Authentication and Firestore, Google Maps/Places, and the OpenAI Responses API.

## Users

Leisure travelers who are either looking for inspiration before choosing a destination or already know where they are going and want ideas for what to do there.

## Product Purpose

WhereTo helps people decide where to travel and what experiences to seek once they arrive. Success means helping a visitor move from uncertainty to a destination or activity idea worth saving and exploring.

## Positioning

WhereTo is a visual travel-discovery product, not a booking funnel. It begins with the visitor's level of certainty and organizes inspiration around destinations and interests.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site supports Firebase accounts, per-user saved places, dynamic destination discovery, live Google place results, and synchronized map pins.
- WhereTo's AI editor can clarify trip preferences, recommend destinations, refine real place results, and suggest areas to stay. OpenAI calls run through the local server so the secret API key is never shipped to the browser.
- A stay can be saved as a home base. Nearby place distance is calculated from that location, and Google Maps can provide a live walking route when available.
- The product remains discovery-focused: no booking flow, multi-day itinerary builder, or automated reservations.
- Reusable content patterns, including destination cards, place cards, stay search, and AI conversation surfaces, are componentized for future expansion.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: WhereTo.
- Voice: inviting, concise, curious, and useful rather than corporate or transactional.
- The product should feel adjacent to Airbnb, Pinterest, and Google Maps while remaining its own discovery experience.

## Evidence on Hand

Destination and place details come from Google Places when configured. AI recommendations are generated by OpenAI and are presented as inspiration that users should verify. No proprietary testimonials, commercial claims, or usage data are available.

## Product Principles

- Begin with the traveler's intent, not an inventory search.
- Make discovery visual and easy to browse.
- Keep choosing a place distinct from planning a booking.
- Let real destination character and useful categories carry the experience.
